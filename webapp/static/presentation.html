03 · keeping the test honest

Same fruit must never sit in train and test

Wrong ideas

Hash whole crop 8×8 → chained 93 different dark avocados into one group

Single 70/15/15 split → only 2 ripe avocados in test

The best ideas

Group: same tree, burst shots, near-identical crops → 1,702 clusters

7-fold stratified group CV: 70% train (1,729), 14% val (345), 16% test (391) with 0% leakage

04 · the method

Ask three yes/no questions

Softmax: 4 unrelated boxes

unripepartially riperiperotten

Cross-entropy only checks “right box or not”.

Ordinal: cumulative targets

0123
grade > 0 ?0111
grade > 1 ?0011
grade > 2 ?0001

Predicted grade = number of “yes” answers.
Being 3 grades off breaks 3 answers, 1 grade off breaks only 1.

04 · decision theory

What do the three grades tell?

Each threshold is an agronomic milestone on the maturation spectrum

Gate 1: P(grade > 0)

Past Unripe?

Physiological Maturation: Chlorophyll degradation and early carotenoid/anthocyanin pigment synthesis.

NO (<50%): Fruit is Unripe (0)
YES (≥50%): Color break has begun
Gate 2: P(grade > 1)

Past Turning?

Edible Ripeness: Starches convert into soluble sugars, flesh softens, aromatic esters peak.

NO (<50%): Partially Ripe (1)
YES (≥50%): Table-ready Ripe (2+)
Gate 3: P(grade > 2)

Past Ripe (Rotten)?

Senescence & Spoilage: Post-harvest degradation, cellular breakdown, dark anthracnose lesions, rot.

NO (<50%): Safe peak Ripe (2)
YES (≥50%): Rotten / Discard (3)
📐 Final Decision Rules: Discrete Grade = count of YES answers (0=Unripe, 1=Turning, 2=Ripe, 3=Rotten). Continuous Score = P(>0) + P(>1) + P(>2). Rank consistency guarantees P(>0) ≥ P(>1) ≥ P(>2) always!

The CORAL head

05 · models & training

A fair, controlled comparison

6 Experiments

Exp A → F · RTX 4050 GPU · 2,465 crops

Exp A: ResNet18 Baseline

Pretrained ResNet18 + CORAL head (MAE 0.599, 16.1% severe errors).

Exp B/C: ResNet50 Softmax

Single-task baselines: Softmax Ripeness has catastrophic 23.3% severe errors.

Exp F: Multi-Task ResNet50

Joint ResNet50: 95.7% Fruit ID + 97.4% Tolerant Ripeness (2.6% severe).

Freeze → Unfreeze

3 epochs head only, then fine-tune backbone at 10× lower LR (1e-5).

RTX 4050 Hardware

NVIDIA RTX 4050 (6GB), FP16 AMP, AdamW (1e-4), Cosine Annealing, 1.08 GB VRAM.

Augmentation

Crop, flips, ±15° rotation, lighting jitter. Never hue – colour is the label.

07 · what we learned

Findings & limits

Multi-Task Synergy

ResNet50 learns shared morphology and peel features: 95.65% Fruit ID & 97.44% Tolerant Ripeness (93.35% dual success).

Ordinal vs Softmax

CORAL bounds mistake distance, slashing catastrophic 2+ grade errors by 9× (23.27% in Softmax → 2.56% in CORAL).

Dual Grad-CAM Attention

Ripeness mode attends to decay lesions and color breaking; Species mode attends to global morphology.

Full-Spectrum Dataset

2,465 validated crops across 6 fruits with all 4 stages represented and 0% data leakage.

RTX 4050 Performance

12ms inference latency per photo, 1.08 GB VRAM footprint under FP16 mixed precision.

Deployment Ready

Interactive Diagnostic Studio, threshold milestone gates, and real-time Grad-CAM explainability.
